With Instagram connected, Direct messages, story replies, and mentions arrive in the same Inbox as your other channels. Your team replies from there, and Tino handles them if you turn on AI for the channel.

<Note>
  Who connects: Administrator, Company Manager, Area Manager, and Unit Manager. Available on all plans; Tino and Campaigns on Instagram require the Scale plan.

  The Tartini interface is in Portuguese. Screen and button names appear here exactly as on screen, with the translation in parentheses.
</Note>

---

## Prepare the Instagram account

Tartini connects through Instagram's official API. Before going to Tartini, check two things on the account:

- **The account is professional.** Business or Creator. In the Instagram app, you change it in **Settings > Account type**. You don't need a Facebook Page.
- **Message access is allowed.** The **Allow access to messages** option must be on, or Direct messages won't reach Tartini.

In the Instagram app, open **Settings and activity** > **Messages and story replies** > **Message requests** and turn on **Allow access to messages**. The option only shows up on a professional account.

---

## Connect the account to Tartini

By default, no Unit has an Instagram channel. Each Unit can have one.

Where to find it: **Configurações > Canais > Instagram** (Settings > Channels > Instagram).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the connection">In the **Atenda o Direct do Instagram no mesmo Inbox** (Handle Instagram Direct in the same Inbox) box, at the top of the screen, click **Conectar Instagram** (Connect Instagram).</Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the channel details">Choose the **Entidade** (Entity) and enter a **Nome do canal** (Channel name) that helps your team identify it later.</Step>
  <Step title="Log in to Instagram">Click **Conectar com Instagram** (Connect with Instagram). The Instagram login screen opens in the same tab: log in with the Unit's professional account and authorize Tartini.</Step>
  <Step title="Check the account">Back in Tartini, the **Instagram conectado** (Instagram connected) screen shows **Conectado como** (Connected as) and the account's @. Click **Ir para o canal** (Go to channel).</Step>
</Steps>

</Frame>

<Note>
  If the login fails or is canceled, the **Não foi possível conectar** (Couldn't connect) screen explains why. Click **Tentar de novo** (Try again) to go back to the channels screen.

## Know what arrives in the Inbox

Instagram conversations arrive in the Inbox like those from other channels. These come in:

- Text, photo, video, audio, and file.
- Reactions and button clicks.
- Story replies and mentions, shared posts and reels. They come in as the media, with a label such as "Respondeu ao seu story" (Replied to your story).
- Messages the person deleted, which show as deleted.

When Instagram doesn't allow some content to be shown, the conversation shows the notice **[Conteúdo que o Instagram não permite exibir aqui]** (Content Instagram doesn't allow to be shown here) in its place.

### How the contact shows up

The contact arrives with name, photo, and @, which links to their Instagram profile. When the person follows your account, the contact record shows **Segue sua empresa** (Follows your company).

<Note>
  Meta only shares the name and photo of people who have already written to your account. The phone number doesn't show, because Instagram doesn't provide it.
</Note>

### The phone number request at closing

When the conversation is closed, if the contact has no phone number and their last message was less than 24 hours ago, Tartini asks whether the person wants to share their number and explains why. Instagram shows a button with the person's own number. If they tap it, the phone number shows up in the contact details.

The request goes out only once per contact. Outside the 24-hour window, it waits for the next closing.

### Message status

"Seen" works: when the person reads it, the message shows as read. Instagram doesn't confirm delivery, so the message goes straight from sent to read.

## Reply on Instagram

Your team replies from the Inbox, as on the other channels. Instagram has a few differences:

- **Plain text.** Instagram has no bold or italics, and the agent's name in the signature goes without bold.
- **Long texts in parts.** Above 1,000 characters, the text is split into more than one message.
- **Attachments.** Photo, video, audio, and PDF go as attachments; other files go as a link. The caption arrives in a message right after the attachment.
- **Reactions.** Only ❤️ is guaranteed.
- **No deleting, editing, or templates.** Tartini can't delete or edit a sent message, and the channel has no templates.

<Note>
  Replies sent from the Instagram app or from Meta Business Suite enter the history as team replies. They don't trigger Tino.
</Note>

## Respect the reply deadline

On Instagram, the person always starts the conversation. Your account can't reach someone who has never written to it, and there is a reply deadline, counted from the contact's last message:

| Time since the contact's last message | Who can reply |
|---|---|
| Up to 24 hours | Tino and the team |
| From 24 hours to 7 days | Only the team. Tino, follow-up, and the satisfaction survey don't send |
| More than 7 days, or the contact never wrote | No one, until the contact writes again |

The Inbox shows the deadline above the message field:

- Up to 24 hours: **Responda em até** (Reply within) and the time left.
- From 24 hours to 7 days: **Passaram 24h: só a equipe pode responder** (24h have passed: only the team can reply), with the time limit.
- After 7 days: the field is locked with **O prazo do Instagram acabou. Aguarde o contato escrever de novo.** (The Instagram deadline is over. Wait for the contact to write again.)

## Turn Tino on for Instagram

With the channel connected, the Instagram screen shows the shortcut to AI per channel. Turn Tino on and set up hours, follow-up, and closing, as on the other channels.

<Note>Available from the Scale plan. See the details in [Turn Tino on in a channel](/en/agentes/tino/ia-por-canal).</Note>

### The notice that the service is automatic

On Instagram, Meta requires automatic service to say it is automatic. That's why the **Aviso de atendimento automatizado** (Automated service notice) block is always on for this channel: you edit the text, but you can't turn it off.

Where to find it: **Configurações > Atendimento > IA por canal** (Settings > Service > AI per channel), on the Instagram channel.

The default text is: "Olá! Sou o assistente virtual da `{empresa}`. Se preferir falar com uma pessoa da equipe, é só pedir." (Hi! I'm the virtual assistant of `{empresa}`. If you'd rather talk to someone on the team, just ask.) `{empresa}` becomes your company's name.

Tino sends the notice at three moments:

- In the first reply of the conversation.
- When the conversation comes back to it from the team.
- After 24 hours without it speaking in the conversation.

<Tip>On the other channels, the same notice exists and is optional.</Tip>

### The satisfaction survey

On Instagram, the satisfaction survey arrives with buttons from 1 to 5. It only goes out within the first 24 hours of the deadline.

## Set up ready-made questions and the menu

Ready-made questions and the persistent menu give the person a quick way to start or continue the conversation without typing.

Where to find it: **Configurações > Canais > Instagram** (Settings > Channels > Instagram), in the channel, section **Perguntas prontas e menu** (Ready-made questions and menu).

- **Ready-made questions:** up to 4, with up to 80 characters each. They show to people who open a conversation with your account for the first time, only in the Instagram phone app.
- **Persistent menu:** up to 5 items, each with a title of up to 30 characters and, optionally, a link starting with `https://`.

Tapping a ready-made question, or a menu item without a link, is the same as sending that text: the message arrives in the Inbox and Tino replies. An item with a link opens the link.

To remove the questions or the menu from the account, leave the list empty and save.

## Find out where the person came from

Tartini records the origin when the person arrives through a Meta ad that leads to Direct, or replies to a message from a [comment trigger](#reply-to-comments-with-a-direct-message):

- The conversation in the Inbox shows the **Veio de anúncio** (Came from an ad) tag, or that the person came from a comment, with the rule name.
- The contact record has the **Origem** (Origin) section, with the history.
- Tino receives this information to understand what brought the person.

<Note>Meta reports the ad origin only once, when the person arrives through that ad.</Note>

## Reply to comments with a Direct message

With comment triggers, whoever comments a keyword on a post or reel from your account gets a private message in Direct. If the person replies, the conversation enters the Inbox and Tino answers.

{/* CONFIRMAR: disponibilidade após App Review de comentários */}

<Note>The feature depends on Meta approving the comments permission and is only available after that. Channels connected before the feature is enabled must be reconnected once to grant this permission; the screen shows a notice when that is needed.</Note>

Where to find it: **Configurações > Canais > Instagram** (Settings > Channels > Instagram), in the channel, section **Gatilhos de comentário** (Comment triggers).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name the rule">The name helps the team recognize the rule and shows as the conversation's origin in the Inbox.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose the post">Pick a post or reel from the account's most recent ones, or **Qualquer post** (Any post).</Step>
  <Step title="Set the keywords">From 1 to 10 words or phrases. The rule matches whole words, ignoring upper and lower case and accents.</Step>
  <Step title="Write the private message">This is the text that reaches the Direct of whoever commented.</Step>
  <Step title="Optionally, reply to the comment in public">A short reply on the comment itself, such as "I sent you a DM". It is optional.</Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The chosen post, the keywords, and the total private messages sent">
  <img src="/images/canais/instagram-gatilhos-comentario.png" alt="Gatilhos de comentário section with an active rule" />
</Frame>

Each rule can be turned on or off, and the screen shows how many private messages it has sent.

When a comment matches more than one rule, the rule for a specific post wins over the **Qualquer post** (Any post) one. Between rules of the same kind, the oldest wins.

On top of that, Tartini sends at most one private message per person on each post. If the same person comments the keyword again on the same post, nothing is sent. If the earlier message failed or its deadline passed, a new comment counts. On a different post, under a **Qualquer post** (Any post) rule, the person gets the message again.

### Meta's rules

- One private message per comment.
- The message only goes out up to 7 days after the comment.
- The conversation only continues if the person replies.
- Up to 750 private messages per hour per account. Above that, messages wait their turn.

### When the person replies

The reply becomes a regular conversation in the Inbox, and Tino answers if AI is on for the channel. The conversation shows as its origin that the person came from a comment, with the rule name. The same origin appears in the **Origem** (Origin) section of the [contact record](/en/inbox/ficha-do-contato), and Tino knows the person arrived through a comment.

<Note>The comments themselves do not enter the Inbox or any other Tartini screen. Comments on lives do not trigger the rules.</Note>

## Campaigns on Instagram

An Instagram campaign only reaches people who have already talked to your account on Instagram and wrote in the last 24 hours. The campaign uses no template and never the team's 7-day window.

<Note>Campaigns require the Scale plan. See the rules and the **Audiência** (Audience) tab in [Create a campaign](/en/campanhas/crie-uma-campanha).</Note>

## Fix channel issues

In the list of connected accounts, the **sem receber** (not receiving) badge shows next to the channel when messages aren't reaching Tartini.

### The channel got disconnected

**Cause:** the Instagram connection expires in 60 days, but Tartini renews it on its own. The channel only drops if someone removes Tartini from the account's connected tools, or if the connection fails.

When that happens, the Unit receives an email warning.

**Fix:** click the ⋮ next to the channel and choose **Reconectar** (Reconnect). Log in with the same account: the history and contacts belong to it, and a different account is refused.

### Another app is the account's main app

**Cause:** the account has another app as the main one for messages, such as Instagram's own Inbox, Meta Business Suite, or another tool. So Tartini doesn't receive the messages.

The Instagram screen shows a yellow warning: **outro app é o principal desta conta do Instagram, e o Tartini não está recebendo as mensagens.** (another app is the main one for this Instagram account, and Tartini isn't receiving the messages.)

**Fix:** set Tartini as the account's main app. The warning shows the path:

- In the Instagram app: **Settings > Messages and story replies > Message controls > Connected tools**.
- In Meta Business Suite: **Settings > Inbox > Connected apps**.

{/* CONFIRMAR: caminho exato para tornar o Tartini o app principal, no app do Instagram e no Meta Business Suite */}

### Meta isn't delivering the messages

**Cause:** Meta stopped delivering the account's messages to Tartini. Tartini checks this every day and resubscribes on its own; the warning shows up when it can't.

The Instagram screen shows a red warning: **a Meta não está entregando as mensagens desta conta ao Tartini.** (Meta isn't delivering this account's messages to Tartini.)

**Fix:** click **Reconectar** (Reconnect) in the warning itself and log in with the same account. The history stays.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Instagram show the contact's phone number?">
  Only if the person shares it. The request with the button reaches them once, when the conversation is closed within the 24 hours.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I send the first message to someone on Instagram?">
  No. The person always starts, with a Direct message, a story reply, or a mention. After that, the team and Tino reply within the deadline.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a Facebook Page?">
  No. The Instagram account just needs to be professional, Business or Creator.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I connect a different account in place of the current one?">
  Not by reconnecting: it only accepts the same account, because the history and contacts belong to it. For a different account, use another Unit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why doesn't the message show as delivered?">
  Instagram doesn't confirm delivery. The message stays as sent and becomes read when the person sees it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did Tino stop replying in a conversation?">
  24 hours have passed since the contact's last message. Up to 7 days, only the team replies; after that, only when the contact writes again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
