The sentence states instead of explaining, brings data instead of adjectives, and the subject is always whoever manages the operation.

---

## Before and after

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| Our platform offers a complete customer service solution. | Your team in charge. |
| Revolutionize your customer service with artificial intelligence. | See everything. Delegate the rest. |
| AI is not here to replace your team. | Who responds, you decide. |

A headline built on negation makes the reader think about what it denies.

---

## Vocabulary

**Words we use:** operation · standard · judgment · monitoring · clarity · a single voice · scale · results · how far the AI goes · people and AI.

**Words we don't use:**

- **automation, automate**: they bring to mind the numbered-menu robot. Instead: agents that work, configure per channel, delegate.
- **flows**: use processes, steps, or workstreams.
- **new era, revolutionize, supercharge, transform**: they take up the line without saying anything.
- **AI technical jargon**: the reader manages customer service.
- **folks, alongside AI**: always say people.

**Names.** Tartini capitalized in running text, tartini lowercase in the lockup. Cérebro, Tino, Copilot, Scout, and Quattro are proper names and are not translated. The two sides are written in lowercase, with a slash: equipe/… and ia/…

## The AI signature

When the artificial intelligence writes, it identifies itself. The block carries a 1.5px dashed cobalt border, a veil in the same blue, and the signature in mono:

```text
ia · copilot

Summary of what has been said in this conversation so far, in three
lines. I am Tartini's artificial intelligence; the reply to the
customer is still up to you.
```
