This guide is for teams that have already connected a channel and taught the Cérebro the minimum, and now want to decide how to start. There are three paths, and the right one depends on your operation: how familiar the team is with AI in customer service, how much volume comes in, and how much material the company already has in the Cérebro.

<Note>
  The Tartini interface is in Portuguese. Button and screen names appear here exactly as on screen, with the translation in parentheses. Options 2 and 3 require Tino, available on the Scale and Enterprise plans. On Essential, option 1 is the path.
</Note>

---

## Option 1: the team answers, the AI supports

Tino stays off. Messages go straight to the team, Copilot answers the questions of whoever is handling the conversation, and Scout evaluates every closed conversation. No customer talks to the AI, and the Cérebro matures with what the team asks Copilot and with the corrections you make in Scout.

**How to do it:** leave **Tino ligado** (Tino on) switched off on every channel, give the team access to Copilot, and turn on **Ativar Scout ao finalizar conversa** (Run Scout when a conversation ends) in **Regras de Conversa** (Conversation Rules).

---

## Option 2: Tino on a test channel

Tino is turned on only in a channel customers do not use yet: a test WhatsApp number connected by QR code, or the Chat Widget installed on an internal page of your site. The team talks to it as if they were customers, and every answer can be checked in **Por que ela disse isso** (Why it said that).

**How to do it:** connect the test channel, turn Tino on only there in **IA por canal** (AI per channel), and use the **Agentes de IA** (AI agents) list in the Inbox to review the conversations.

---

## Option 3: Tino for a short window

Tino is turned on in a real channel, but only during a period when someone on the team is around to take over, for example two hours a day. The rest of the day stays with the team, and any conversation can be taken over with **Interromper** (Interrupt). The window grows as confidence grows.

**How to do it:** in **IA por canal**, choose **Personalizado** (Custom) under **Quando Tino atende** (When Tino answers) and set the period. Leave follow-up off for the first few days.

---

## Which path to take

If you have never worked with AI in customer service, we recommend starting with option 1: the team answers with Copilot at its side. That is how the team gets used to the AI without exposing customers, and how the Cérebro matures, through the team's questions and the manager's corrections in Scout. Once the Cérebro is more mature, connect Tino to a channel and see the scale Tartini offers through its agents.

Teams that already operate with AI, or that have a ready and reviewed knowledge base, can go straight to option 2 and, after a few days, to option 3.

---

## Before turning Tino on for customers

Check these points, in order:

1. **The Identity is complete.** The checklist on the Home page shows 100 percent: properties, communication, guidelines and compliance.
2. **Operations are configured.** Hours in Availability, at least one Routing rule and the Clarifications Tino should ask before acting.
3. **The knowledge answers real questions.** Test the ten most common customer questions in the Cérebro. See [Test what the AI has learned](/cerebro/teste-o-conhecimento).
4. **Someone will watch the first conversations.** The **Agentes de IA** list in the Inbox and Scout show what Tino did. Reserve one person for this in the first week.
5. **Credits are enough.** Check the balance and the projection in **Configurações > Cobrança > Créditos** (Settings, Billing, Credits).
