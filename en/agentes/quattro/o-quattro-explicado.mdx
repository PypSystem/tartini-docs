Quattro is the AI agent that writes the operation down. You describe, in your own words, how a service interaction happens today: a duplicate invoice, a booking, an exchange. Quattro asks what's missing and builds the process, with steps, transitions, and outcomes. Nothing it builds takes effect without your approval.

<Note>
  Quattro is in **Cérebro > Processos > Mapeamento** (Cérebro > Processes > Mapping). Mapping is done by the Administrator or the Company Manager. Processes are part of the Scale and Enterprise plans, and the conversation with Quattro consumes AI credits.

  The Tartini interface is in Portuguese. Screen and button names appear here exactly as on screen, with the translation in parentheses.
</Note>

---

## How it works

Mapping a process isn't filling out a form: it's a conversation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="You describe">In **Cérebro > Processos > Mapeamento**, click **Novo processo** (New process) or one of the example cards, and describe how the service interaction happens.</Step>
  <Step title="Quattro asks">It asks for what's missing: who does it, when, what happens at each outcome. Next to the conversation, a panel shows the process filling in in real time.</Step>
  <Step title="It measures capacity">With the step-by-step ready, **Avaliar capacidade** (Assess capacity) measures what Tino resolves on its own at each step.</Step>
  <Step title="You review and publish">In **Revisar e publicar** (Review and publish), you check the 5W2H, the coverage, and the steps, adjust the suggested outcomes, and click **Publicar processo** (Publish process).</Step>
</Steps>

See the full step-by-step in [Map processes from conversations](/en/agentes/quattro/mapeie-processos).

## What it builds

Each process brings together:

- **5W2H**: the what, why, who, when, where, and how much of that service interaction.
- **Passo a passo** (Step-by-step): the steps Tino follows, with the text it sends at each one.
- **Saídas** (Outcomes): where the conversation goes at each ending, such as Concluído (Completed), Parcial (Partial), Falhou (Failed), or Faltou dado (Missing data).
- **Cobertura do Tino** (Tino's coverage): how much of the process Tino resolves on its own, step by step.
- **Ferramentas** (Tools): a step that uses one of the company's connectors, for example to check a system, is included too. You choose which tools are available for that process. See [Connect external tools to the AI](/en/integracoes/conectores).

## What happens after publishing

The published process takes effect on both sides of the service interaction:

- **Tino follows it.** During the conversation, it uses the process that matches the customer's request, step by step, and follows the outcome set for that ending.
- **Scout checks it.** It audits real conversations against what was mapped and shows what Tino resolved on its own, where it needed help, and where what was expected didn't match what happened. See [See Process Adherence](/en/agentes/scout/processos-e-aderencia).

To change a published process, use **Editar conteúdo** (Edit content) to adjust only the text, or **Editar com o Quattro** (Edit with Quattro) to change the structure: add, remove, or reorder steps.

---

## Discoveries

Quattro also prepares the documentation from what the company already has. Process candidates identified in content indexed in the Cérebro, or in documents sent just for this analysis, arrive in **Cérebro > Processos > Descobertas** (Cérebro > Processes > Discoveries). You open the mapping to review it, approve it, approve and publish it already active, or discard it. A process created from a discovery starts out inactive, unless you choose to publish it active.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between Quattro and Tino?">
  Quattro leads the mapping conversation with you. Tino serves the actual customer, following the process that's already published.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does saving without publishing leave Tino using the process?">
  No. Saving without publishing creates or updates the process as inactive and doesn't save the outcomes. The process only goes into operation once it's published.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the steps without talking to Quattro?">
  No. Adding, removing, or reordering steps, and changing gates and conditions, is only possible through the conversation with Quattro. The content editor is only for text that already exists.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Quattro consume credits?">
  Yes. The mapping conversation consumes AI credits, like other interactions with the agents.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
